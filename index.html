Terrain chip: bottom of the pile, +1 defense each. Modifiers stack: hills + big river + city = 1 + 2 + 1 = 4 chips, each coloured by its modifier Forest (Ardennes, Argonne...) +1 Hills (Eifel, Chemin des Dames, Vimy...) +1 Marsh (Flanders polders, Rhine delta...) +1 Mountains (Vosges crest) +2 River (a blue line; the hexes it runs through) +1 Big river (Rhine, lower Meuse, Scheldt estuary) +2 City +1 Big city (Paris, Cologne, Essen, Brussels, Amsterdam) +2 Fortress (Verdun, Liege, Metz, Strasbourg...) +2 Sea: impassable Trench chip: above terrain, +1 defense each France (Player A) Germany (Player B) Belgium, Luxembourg, the Netherlands: neutral ground, nobody's territory (borders are the pale lines along hex edges) Gear / experience chip Spent stack (just advanced): -1 strength until its owner's next turn One men chip = 30,000 soldiers (about a corps). Armies start where they stood in August 1914: French 1st-5th Armies in France, German 1st-7th Armies in Germany (about 1.0 and 1.4 million men) Stack label "6/4": 6 = defending strength (terrain, trenches, brace included), 4 = attacking strength (men + gear) Cut off (no path home that avoids enemy zones): cannot act or retreat, loses 1 gear, then 1 man each turn Enemy zone of control: free movement cannot enter it; an assault can (2 points, or -2 strength) Assault entry hex (pick it, then the enemy to hit)
