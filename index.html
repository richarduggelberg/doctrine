Terrain

Terrain chip: bottom of the pile, +1 defense each. Modifiers stack: hills + big river + city = 1 + 2 + 1 = 4 chips, each coloured by its modifier Forest (Ardennes, Argonne...) +1 Hills (Eifel, Chemin des Dames, Vimy...) +1 Marsh (Flanders polders, Rhine delta...) +1 Mountains (Vosges crest) +2 River (a blue line; the hexes it runs through) +1 Big river (Rhine, lower Meuse, Scheldt estuary) +2 City +1 Big city (Paris, Cologne, Essen, Brussels) +2 Fortress (Verdun, Liege, Metz, Strasbourg...) +2 Sea: impassable Trench chip: above terrain, +1 defense each. A hex with no stack keeps at most one chip Line: a defender gets +1 for each friendly stack on an adjacent hex (max +2). Stacks side by side hold better than lone ones; the attacker gets nothing Recruits: at the start of your turn you muster 1 / 1 / 2 / 3 recruits (Willingness doctrine level 0-3). Click a highlighted empty town on your own soil (not enemy-held, not surrounded) to place each as a new 1-man stack. Unplaced recruits are lost Actions: each stack gets one action a turn: walk, shell, assault/grind, merge or split. A stack that walked cannot fight, and one that fought cannot walk. Trenches, rally, relief, recruits and rail are not actions; forced moves (a retreat, the advance after a win) are free Assault: you need strictly more strength (men + gear) than the defender's men + gear + terrain + trench chips + support. It always costs you 2 men; the defender retreats and you advance. A defender that cannot retreat (every exit holds a stack or lies in an enemy zone) is destroyed. Failed or tied assaults are not allowed: grind or shell instead Grind: an attack that is not a win costs you 1 man and nobody moves; the defender's hex loses 1 trench chip, and once it has none the defender loses 1 man too. A lone man cannot be ground below 1 (shelling stops at 2 men), only assaulted. Every attack, assault or grind, costs 1 point (none with Combined arms) Artillery: the shooter must be adjacent to its target and shelling is its action. With an Offensive order, pay 1 point to strip a trench chip from the target's hex (down to 1 chip), then 2 points per man (1 with Combined arms) at no cost to yours; never below 2 men France (Player A) Germany (Player B) Belgium, Luxembourg, the Netherlands: neutral ground, nobody's territory (borders are the dark lines along hex edges) Gear / experience chip One men chip = 30,000 soldiers (about a corps). Armies start where they stood in August 1914: French 1st-5th Armies in France, German 1st-7th Armies in Germany (about 1.0 and 1.4 million men) Stack label "6/4": 6 = defending strength (terrain, trenches and adjacent support included), 4 = attacking strength (men + gear) Surrounded / cut off (no path of up to 8 hexes to the capital or a held town that avoids enemy zones; touching friendly stacks pass supply along; a stack on a held town outside enemy zones is supplied by it): cannot act, exerts no zone of control, loses 1 gear chip, then 1 man, at the start of each of its owner's turns Towns: ~100 named places, bigger label = bigger place. A town on French or German soil taken by the enemy gets a flag and a dashed outline in the captor's colour; every turn its owner gains +1 exhaustion per town lost (see Exhaustion). Standing on it again retakes it Exhaustion: each nation has a public meter with a fixed limit (55). It fills with every man lost, +1 every turn, and the town ticks above. Home Front relief lowers it. A full meter loses the war Front line: each side draws the edge of the hexes nearer to its stacks than to the enemy's (red = A, blue = B); hexes equally near belong to nobody. It shifts as stacks advance and fall back. Toggle it in the header Enemy zone of control: entering it is free but ends that stack's walk, as does entering a hex with terrain or trench chips (rail ignores the latter, on home soil); a stack inside a zone can only step out Cards: a normal 52-card deck per player (no jokers). ♠ Offensive (only this suit attacks), ♣ Defence, ♦ Logistics, ♥ Home Front. Ranks 2-4 base, 5-7 +1, 8-10 +2, J-K +3, Ace +4; doctrine shifts the tier up or down (it can go above the printed band). Any number of same-suit cards can be played together as a set for one stronger order: their points add up, plus 1 per extra card; faces and aces can join but lose their abilities. Play as many orders as you have cards: you start with 4 cards, draw 2 at the end of each turn, and hold at most 7, so saving cards builds up an offensive. You can discard any number of cards, but it does nothing (no replacement); it only makes room under the cap. Improvise: discard any two cards of the same suit together to play a plain rank-2 order of any suit (the cards lose their abilities). J, Q, K and A have special abilities
